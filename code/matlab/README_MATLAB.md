# MATLAB figure scripts

## Fig7_emission_sink_pressure_final.m

Reproduces the main-text Fig. 7:

- Panel (a): 2060 remaining industrial emissions by subsector and the
  2001-2020 terrestrial ecosystem sink benchmark.
- Panel (b): industrial emission pressure ratios under terrestrial sink
  benchmarks equal to 80%, 100%, and 120% of the historical benchmark.

The sector-level values match the uploaded final Fig. 7 script and the
reported regional totals used in the manuscript.

## FigS1_temporal_completeness_sensitivity.m

Reproduces Supporting Information Fig. S1:

- Panel (a): terrestrial sink benchmarks under minimum valid-observation
  thresholds of 5, 10, and 15 years.
- Panel (b): the corresponding industrial emission pressure ratios.

Both scripts export 600 dpi PNG and vector PDF files.
